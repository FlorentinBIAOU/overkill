The entries give you the reasoning and the code. Going from there to something running in
your own infrastructure, with your data and your constraints, takes more than that. And
sometimes no entry matches what you have in front of you.

Either way, write to me.

## What I’m interested in

**A use case the catalogue doesn’t cover.** You have a model call in production, or you are
about to add one, and no entry addresses that need. That is exactly what the catalogue is
missing: tell me which one, and it will end up there.

**A reasoned disagreement.** An entry recommends a level your experience contradicts. Those
are the most useful messages, and they end up in the content.

**An error.** A snippet that no longer runs, a figure that looks wrong, a breaking point that
isn’t one. Corrections come before additions.

## What I don’t do here

No vendor recommendations, no reselling, and no regulatory compliance work, which is a
lawyer’s job.

And no answer written in advance. If your case genuinely needs a general-purpose model, that
is what I will tell you.

## Writing

Say in a few lines what the task does, what runs today, and what makes you doubt it. That is
enough for a first exchange.
