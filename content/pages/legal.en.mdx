## Source code and content

The site's code and the entries' content are public. The repository is the record: it carries

## Reporting content

A factual error on an entry can be reported by opening an issue on the repository, or by
writing to the contact address. Corrections on substance come before additions.

## Limitation of liability

The entries on this site describe technical approaches and their known limits. They are
neither a guarantee of results, nor legal advice, nor an audit of your particular situation.

The "regulatory scope" block on each rung is general information, kept up to date as best we
can, and dated. It tells nobody that they are compliant or not. Have a professional review
your situation.
