## What this site does not do

**No cookies are set.** No session cookie, no technical cookie, no preference cookie. No
consent banner is needed, because there is nothing to consent to.

**No trackers are loaded.** No analytics, no pixel, no browser fingerprinting, no heatmap, no
session recording.

**No third-party resource is loaded at runtime.** Fonts are served from this domain. There is
no remote library, no web font service, no external image, no embedded video player. Your
browser contacts no server other than this one during your visit.

**No accounts exist.** No sign-up, no log-in, no profile, no user database. The site is a set of
static files.

**No personal data is collected.** No form, no comment, no input field sent anywhere. The
catalogue search runs entirely in your browser: what you type goes nowhere.

## What is stored on your device

Two preferences, and only if you express them explicitly:

| Key | What it holds | When it is written |
|---|---|---|
| `overkill-theme` | `light` or `dark` | only if you click the theme toggle |
| `overkill-lang` | `fr` or `en` | only if you click the language switch |

These values live in your browser's local storage. They are **never** transmitted, they contain
no identifier, and they are not written until you have chosen something. Clearing the site's
data removes them.

## The only possible processing

If you write to the address given on the [help](/en/accompagnement) page, your message and your
email address arrive in an ordinary mailbox.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Purpose** | to reply to your message |
| **Legal basis** | your own initiative in writing |
| **Recipient** | the site's publisher, nobody else |
| **Retention** | three years after the last exchange, then deletion |
| **Your rights** | access, rectification, erasure, objection, on request to that same address |

Your address is added to no mailing list, passed to nobody, and used for nothing else.

## The host's logs

Like any server, this site's host keeps technical logs containing your IP address, to run the
service and defend against abuse. That processing is the host's, whose identity appears on the
[legal notice](/en/mentions-legales) page. We have no access to it and derive no statistics
from it.

## If that ever changes

We have no analytics and do not want any. If knowing the volume ever became necessary, the
measurement would be **cookie-free, without individual identifiers and aggregated**, it would
be announced here before being put in place, and this page would carry a new date.

## Why this page says something

On a site that talks about frugality and regulatory risk, the total absence of trackers is not
a formality, it is a demonstration. Every tracker a site adds is a decision: someone judged
that knowing your behaviour was worth more than leaving you alone. Here, that decision was not
taken.

You can check for yourself. Open your browser's network tab and reload: you will see only this
domain.
